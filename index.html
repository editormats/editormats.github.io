Technical Pipeline

How It's Built

A proprietary production pipeline from virtual set capture through final composite. Every stage controlled in-house.

01

Virtual Set Capture

Treating virtual data environments as a multi-camera studio to extract dynamic cinematic angles. The replay system is rewound, repositioned, and reconstructed to find angles that don't exist in live play.

02

Automation & Tool Development

Engineered a custom Luau-based mathematical camera scripting tool to automate sub-frame movements, field-of-view translation, and dynamic motion curves. Full control over position, rotation, FOV, and depth-of-field.

03

Pre-Render Real-Time Grading

Deploying custom engine shaders, ambient occlusion mapping, and color spaces prior to post-production compositing. Every shot is graded, sharpened, and bloom-tuned before footage leaves the virtual environment.

04

After Effects

The compositing stage: 3D camera tracking, VFX layering, rotoscoping, and environment blends. Turning virtual footage into something that looks designed, not recorded.

05

Blender

When a shot requires true 3D space: object animation, scene construction, and depth integration that can't be achieved with compositing alone.

06

Final Cut

Colour, sound design, and pacing locked to the music. Nothing ships until it looks right.

About

The Designer

I'm Mats Koning, a video editor and motion designer specializing in high-energy short-form content. I handle my entire production pipeline from scratch, utilizing After Effects, Blender, and 3D camera tracking.

To push my work further, I built custom Luau-based camera automation to create precise camera paths that standard capture software can't reach.

I'm an official creator for Another Axiom, and I focus on turning raw digital assets into cinematic videos that pull numbers and hold an audience's attention.
