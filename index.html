Technical Pipeline

How It's Built

A proprietary production pipeline from virtual set capture through final composite. Every stage controlled in-house.

01

Virtual Set Capture

I treat digital game environments like a real-world photo studio. By rewinding and repositioning internal replay files, I can capture dramatic angles that are impossible to get during live gameplay.

02

Automation & Tool Development

Standard recording software is highly restrictive. To solve this, I coded my own camera tool using Luau. It automates smooth camera paths, custom field-of-view shifts, and gives me complete control over depth-of-field and framing.

03

Pre-Render Real-Time Grading

Before moving any footage to editing software, I tweak game shaders, set up ambient occlusion, and adjust color spaces. This ensures the raw video looks sharp, vibrant, and cinematic before post-production even begins.

04

After Effects

This is where everything comes together. I use After Effects for 3D camera tracking, layered visual effects, rotoscoping, motion graphics and blending environments so the final video looks like a movie, not a screen recording.

05

Blender

When a shot needs deeper perspective, I move into Blender. This allows me to animate custom objects, build full 3D sets, and integrate realistic depth that compositing alone cannot replicate.

06

Final Cut

The final step. I lock down the color grading, mix custom sound effects, and tighten the pacing to the music so every cut hits perfectly.

About

The Designer

I’m Mats Koning, a video editor and motion designer specializing in high-energy, cinematic short-form content. I handle my entire production process from scratch using After Effects, Blender, and 3D tracking.

Standard recording and spectator software were too limiting for the concepts I wanted to create. To get around those restrictions, I coded my own camera automation system using Luau. This gives me total control over framing and allows me to build complex, smooth camera movements that aren't possible with basic capture software.

Because of the quality and style of my content, I am recognized as an official creator for Another Axiom—the studio behind Gorilla Tag and Orion Drift. My focus is strictly on transforming raw virtual assets into polished, cinematic videos that capture a viewer's attention and do justice to the games.
