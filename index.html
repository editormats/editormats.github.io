Behind the Work

How It's Made

Every shot is constructed, not captured. I treat replays as raw material and the spectator camera as a tool I fully control.

01

Replay System

Orion Drift's replay viewer is where everything starts. I rewind, reposition, and reconstruct moments — finding angles that don't exist in live play.

02

Custom Animator Script

I built a camera animator (Luau-based) that lives inside the spectator system. Full control over position, rotation, FOV, DoF and more. Every camera move is intentional.

03

ReShade

Custom colour pipeline tuned per-shot. Grading, ambient occlusion, bloom, sharpening — everything that happens before footage leaves the game.

04

After Effects

This is where cinematic layers build — compositing, camera tracking, VFX, environment blends. Turning game footage into something that looks like it was shot, not recorded.

05

Blender

When a shot needs to exist in 3D space. Object animation, scene construction, depth that doesn't exist in-game.

06

Final Cut

Colour, sound design, pacing. Every cut locks to the music. If it doesn't hit on the beat, it's wrong.

About

The Editor

I'm Mats — a cinematic editor and top competitive Orion Drift player. I construct shots, not capture them. Using a custom camera animator tooll built into the game's spectator system, I treat every replay like a director's cut.

The workflow runs from in-game camera scripting through a ReShade pipeline, into After Effects for compositing, and Blender when the shot needs dimension. I notice when something's off and don't ship until it looks right.

Competing for team COLD. Posting everything at @editormats.
